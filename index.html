02

The recovery protocol

A plan that advances as your body does.

Not a fixed weekly menu. You set a recovery profile — injury, surgery dates, rehab stage, appetite — and the plan moves through three medically-defined phases, adjusting protein, calories and texture at each step.

01

Wound healing

High protein, zinc, iron, vitamins C & D to rebuild tissue and blood. Soft, calorie-dense meals for a suppressed appetite.

02

Rehabilitation

As physiotherapy ramps up, protein timing protects muscle against immobilisation loss. Anti-inflammatory, omega-3 emphasis.

03

Coming home

Calories step down from hospital levels so weight stays steady after discharge. Everyday Ukrainian home cooking returns.

flashlight_on

Blackout mode

Every phase has versions that need only a kettle or one burner — for shelling, outages, or a hospital ward.

supervisor_account

Caregiver mode

Built to be run by whoever is cooking — a spouse, a parent — not only the person recovering.

One protocol, three starting points.

The recovery profile asks where you're starting from — and the plan begins at the right phase for you.

restaurant

Everyday Ukrainians

Living through a fourth year of war means broken sleep, skipped meals and stress eating — even far from the front. You start at Phase 3: steady, familiar home cooking that holds a routine together.

Starts at Phase 3 · Coming home

military_tech

Veterans returning home

After discharge, hospital-level rations no longer fit a civilian day, and appetite, sleep and weight all shift. You start at Phase 2 and step down — with appetite- and PTSD-aware design that hides numbers when they hurt.

Starts at Phase 2 · Rehabilitation

healing

Heavily injured & their families

Amputation, burns, blast injury or long immobilisation change what the body needs to heal. Enter the discharge summary and Phase 1 begins — soft, high-protein meals, run in Caregiver mode by whoever is cooking.

Starts at Phase 1 · Wound healing
